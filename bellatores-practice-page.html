The club

Every warrior started
on borrowed ground.

Bellatores FC began as a Sunday kickabout on a dirt lot with tyre-lined goalposts. No changing rooms, no floodlights — just a ball, a chalk line, and eleven people who refused to stop showing up.

A decade on, we've traded the dirt for turf, but the standard hasn't moved: play for who's next to you, earn the shirt every week, and never let the badge down.
